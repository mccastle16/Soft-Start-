Soft Start · Visual pass 05

Gentle Interruptions

Everything that could feel like a warning, designed to feel like a question instead — never red, never bold alarm.

Three coloring candidates for the question voice — the frames below wear A; the strip shows all three:

A · Deep blush. Fully in the rose family — a shade deeper than any surface, dot in deep rose. Quietest option.
B · Dusty mauve. A whisper of violet in the pink — distinct from every surface, still soft and cool.
C · Terracotta-cream. The previous round — warm cousin of the family.

Whichever wins keeps the exclusivity rule: this tone appears nowhere in the app except when it's gently asking you something.

N1/N2 inline notes slide in place between blocks: apricot-cream card, one-tap fix in a soft white pill, a quieter dismiss. N2 (boundary) is the same component with different words and a warmer shadow on the block that caused it.

R3a is a small centered card, not a full-screen takeover — two equal blush answers, neither styled as "correct."

SH4 lists only what genuinely can't fit; each row holds its own Move/Rest pair (a chosen answer turns rose), and the sheet won't rush you.
