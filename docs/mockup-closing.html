Rhythm & the Closing States

Rhythm reuses the settings-row pattern from first-run: weekday rows with block counts, weekend rows resting in blush ("blank by default"), then Day shape and Data & backup.

Evening shows only what happened — titles already quieted to gray, sage checks doing the only sage in the app — and always closes on the warm line.

The celebration lives inside the evening state — not a separate screen, and no ring animation. On a 100% day the same sheet simply says "Wow! What a day!" below the warm line — the only exclamation mark in the entire app, spent on pure joy — while rose petals drift down from the top of the screen. Reduced-motion: the line alone.
