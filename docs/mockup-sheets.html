Sheets — SH1 + SH2

Sheets rise over a gently dimmed, softly blurred Today — the day stays visible behind everything you do to it. Warm-ink scrim (never gray-black), 28px top corners, a rose-tinted upward shadow.

SH1 hierarchy is the product philosophy in button form: Done is the one rose action; Move/Rest sit beneath as equal blush twins; Edit block is a ghost. The time row is the blush shortcut — tap it to retime inline.

SH2: title field → category pills (selected = rose fill) → time row defaulting to the next open gap → the rhythm toggle, quiet and off by default. Permanence is a deliberate flick, never an accident.
