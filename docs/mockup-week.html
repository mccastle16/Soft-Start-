S2 — Week

Orientation, never a report card. Four card temperatures: past days recede fully into gray — even their done marks — with only the sage "done" tag and the "showed up for" line keeping a hint of life; today floats highest with its blush tag; future weekdays preview their rhythm in the rose-and-cream category family (widths ∝ duration, anchors ringed); weekends glow in a blush gradient with the rose "Add something" pill.

Color semantics: categories speak only rose and cream. Sage speaks exactly once — the word "done." Apricot speaks only when the app asks a question. Functional colors never compete with decorative ones.

Undone things on past days are simply not there — the gray marks that exist are the day's whole story, resting.
